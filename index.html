        Where the data comes from. Blizzard's WoW Forever Armory, read through Classic Armory about once an hour.

        How the math works. XP per level comes from the Classic XP table: 4,084,700 XP from level 1 to 60. Play time per level is estimated from that XP divided by how fast XP comes in at that level, which rises with level the way mob XP does (45 + 5 × level per kill). The projection assumes he keeps the same daily play time as his recent pace. The Armory doesn't show XP inside a level, so every count starts at the beginning of his current level.
