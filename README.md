# bonitabluehair.com

Can Bonita Bluehair hit level 60 in WoW: Forever before Christmas? This site keeps score.

## Open items (as of Nov 9, 2026)

- **Site:** `index.html` reads `data/history.json` and draws everything in the browser.
- **Updates:** `.github/workflows/update.yml` calls `scripts/update.py`, which reads the character from the WoW Forever Armory (through classicarmory.gg) and retries through rate limits. It records level snapshots, saves a new gear-reel frame whenever his equipped gear or his render changes, commits, and redeploys GitHub Pages (only when something was saved). The Armory redraws his picture later than it updates his gear, so a gear change is saved right away with the Armory's last picture (`"stalePic": true`, with a note on the page) and the new picture is swapped into that frame once the Armory catches up. When an Armory response leaves out professions, reputations or parts of the record, the last known values are kept instead of being wiped.
- **The hourly clock:** GitHub's own schedule starts the workflow hours late or skips it (Nov 8–9: every 4 to 7 hours, then nothing for 9), so the Cloudflare Worker in `votes-worker/` starts it at :07 every hour with a cron trigger. GitHub's schedule stays on as a backup and does nothing when the last check was under 40 minutes ago. It has been on since Nov 9, 2026 (first run 23:07 UTC). The token expires on the date chosen when it was made; when it does, the hourly runs stop and the Worker's cron runs show as failed, so redo steps 1 and 2. Setup:
  1. On GitHub: Settings → Developer settings → Fine-grained tokens → Generate new token. Repository access: only `TwinkieSlamer/bonitabluehair`. Permissions: Actions → Read and write. Set the expiry past Christmas.
  2. From `votes-worker/`: `npx wrangler@4.152.0 secret put GITHUB_DISPATCH_TOKEN` and paste the token, then `npx wrangler@4.152.0 deploy`.
  3. Check it: within the hour the Actions tab shows a "workflow_dispatch" run at about :07. Cloudflare dashboard → Workers → bonita-votes → Logs shows each cron run; a failed one means the token is missing, wrong or expired.
- **Run an update now:** Actions tab → "Update tracker and deploy" → Run workflow.
- **Live XP (optional):** `kit/` holds the XPReport addon (level and XP only) and the XP uploader. The uploader posts `live.json` to the separate `TwinkieSlamer/bonitabluehair-live` repo whenever WoW saves (logout or /reload), and the race page reads it from there. The Armory stays the referee: if a later Armory logout shows a lower level than the addon claimed, the page ignores the addon. Download for players: `downloads/xpreport-kit.zip`.
- **The vote ("Does he make it?"):** the race page reads and saves calls through a small Cloudflare Worker in `votes-worker/`, at https://bonita-votes.votes-worker.workers.dev, backed by a D1 database. Names are optional. The server stamps each call with his level from this site's data, closes voting at 60 or Christmas, and caps new votes at 20 per network per day. Redeploy with `npx wrangler@4.152.0 deploy` from that folder; test locally with `wrangler dev --port 8787` and open the page at `http://localhost:8765/?votes=local`.
- **The 3D stage:** on, with `models/bonita-2026-11-09.glb` (0.4 MB, his level 32 gear), exported with wow.export 0.2.21 from a local WoW: Forever install: Night Elf female, appearance matched to the Armory render by eye (the Armory doesn't record it), gear from the Armory's item IDs, idle clip only. wow.export leaves the staff and shoulders unattached at the origin; `tools/model/fix-attach.mjs` puts the staff on its hand bone and the shoulder pads on the collarbones (on the upper-arm attachment bones they swing down with the arm), and turns the model to face the camera. wow.export also doesn't load cape textures for this client, so the cape comes out wearing the body's texture; the script takes the cape's own texture as a PNG and gives the cape its own material (look the texture up from the item: ItemModifiedAppearance → ItemAppearance → ItemDisplayInfo → its material resource → the `item/objectcomponents/cape/` file, and save it as PNG from wow.export's Textures tab). The idle always plays, like the race chart, and pauses only off screen. This is extracted game art, which Blizzard has warned can draw takedowns; to turn it off, set `"model": null` in the manifest. The page only marks the model stale when a newer gear-reel frame lists different gear than the manifest's `gear`. When `models/manifest.json` has a `"model"`, the race page loads `vendor/model-viewer-4.4.0.min.js` (Google, Apache 2.0; meshopt decoder from meshoptimizer 1.2.0, MIT; both checked byte for byte against npm) after the page has loaded, fades the model in over the render, and lets people turn him by drag or arrow keys. It falls back to the render on any failure, offers "Load 3D" on Save-Data or slow connections (or always, with `"autoload": false`), and says "still has his {date} gear" when the gear reel has a newer frame than `exportedAt`. To turn it on:
  1. Export his model as glTF with animations (wow.export, on a PC with WoW: Forever installed; Forever support isn't confirmed). Using a model taken from the game files is the owner's call: Blizzard has warned that datamined assets can draw takedowns.
  2. `cd tools/model && npm install`, then `npx gltf-transform copy bonita.gltf raw.glb`, `node keep-idle.mjs raw.glb idle.glb 1` (clip 1 is Stand), `node fix-attach.mjs idle.glb fixed.glb cape.png` (leave the PNG off if he has no cape; the script warns about any loose piece it doesn't know how to attach), and `npx gltf-transform optimize fixed.glb bonita-YYYY-MM-DD.glb --compress meshopt --texture-compress webp --texture-size 1024 --simplify false --flatten false --join false --instance false --palette false` (aim for under 1 MB).
  3. Put the `.glb` in `models/` (and delete the old one), then fill in `manifest.json`: `model`, `bytes`, `exportedAt`, `gearAsOf`, `gear` (the item names from the newest look in `data/history.json`), and the framing (`orientation`, `cameraOrbit`, `cameraTarget`, `fieldOfView`) so his feet land on the ground disc and his head in the halo ring. On localhost, `?model=`, `?look=YYYY-MM-DD` and `?sim=savedata|nowebgl` preview the other states.
  4. Export again whenever the gear reel saves a new frame.
- **Day and night sky:** both page headers follow the viewer's local time of day: the sun's height is worked out from the date and clock (40°N, solar noon at 12:00), the sky blends through night, blue hour, dawn/dusk, golden hour and day, and the moon sets as the sun rises in the same corner, clear of the text. Hero labels lighten in daylight to keep their contrast. Preview another time on localhost with `?sky=HH:MM`.
- **Change the deadline:** edit `deadline` in `data/history.json` (ISO time, UTC). It's set to midnight Eastern on Dec 25.

Pace math uses the Classic XP table, with play time per level estimated as XP ÷ (45 + 5 × level).
