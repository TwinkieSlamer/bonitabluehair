How the hourglass works. The sand runs from launch, Nov 4 at 6:00 PM ET, to the deadline, Dec 25 at 12:00 AM ET: 50 days and 6 hours. The top bulb always holds the share of that time that's left, measured by the sand's volume, so it drains in real time, about 2% a day. The clock is your device's clock.

His level comes from the same hourly check of Blizzard's character data as the race.
